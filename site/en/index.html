S.03CONTROL SYSTEM

REDUCED-GRAVITY MOTION

Dual-Axis Gravity Controller

Dual-axis continuous-rotation servo control using angle feedback, velocity estimation, feedforward, and PID to study motion that changes the gravity-vector direction over time.

  • ESP32-C3
  • Feedback 360
  • PID
Purpose, approach, and scope
Purpose
Control dual-axis rotation angles and speeds for research involving time-averaged gravity-vector orientation.
Approach
Read servo angle feedback with ESP32-C3, estimate velocity, and apply feedforward compensation and PID control.
Scope
Rotation is not true weightlessness. Sample position, rotation speed, and centrifugal acceleration affect the experimental conditions.
